## Notes

**How the price is built (as far as the screen shows)**
- **Multizones** is the zone product. It is offered only where every stop lies in the zones 10 / 200 / 210 / 230 / 240 / 250. It prints a zone list and a "via" (the via does not change the price). Prices seen, adult 2nd class:

  | Zones | Annual | Monthly |
  |---|---|---|
  | 10 + one of 200 / 230 / 240 / 250 | 781.60 | 101.30 |
  | 10 + 210 | 914.00 | 111.40 |
  | 10 + 210 + a second ring zone | 1195.60 | 142.70 |

- **Zone 210 costs more than the other ring zones.** It is not a flat per-zone price: 10+210 costs 132.40 a year more than 10+230. I did not see a combination of two non-210 ring zones, so I cannot say whether 10+230+250 also costs 1195.60.
- **Supplement over unireso.** The shop shows no unireso-only price on any French-side trip, so the supplement on top of a unireso pass can't be read from these screens. The only zone-10-alone price seen is the Swiss modular "incl. Genève [10]" at 1497.00 / 179.00 (Coppet) and 1864.00 / 220.00 (Nyon). That is a modular add-on price, not a standalone unireso pass.
- **Minimum zone count.** Every Léman Pass Multizones pass includes zone 10, so the minimum is 2 zones. There is no French-only (single-ring) pass, even for Annemasse → Gaillard.
- **Route pricing.** It applies to Valleiry, Thonon, Évian, Annecy, Bellegarde, La Roche-sur-Foron, Reignier and Bonneville. Products are named "Modular Travelcard" or "Point-to-point Travelcard", and the ticket line reads "Léman Pass Season Ticket", "Léman Pass (271)/(272) Season Ticket" (Alpbus) or "Léman Pass TER Season Ticket (only route section)".
  - The rail Modular price via Annemasse – Genève-Eaux-Vives is always exactly 607.00 a year (57.60 a month) below the Point-to-point price on the same route. This holds for Saint-Julien, Valleiry, La Roche, Reignier and Bonneville, and the gap reads like zone 10 being bundled differently. The shop does not say why.
  - Where the modular panel offers variants, adding the outer zone costs:

    | Outer zone | Annual | Monthly |
    |---|---|---|
    | 300, from Thonon | +475.30 | +45.10 |
    | 380, Annecy | +335.80 | +41.00 |
    | 10 on top of 400, Bellegarde | +500.00 | +70.00 |

- **Annual is not monthly × 9 for Léman Pass.** The annual/monthly ratio is 8.20 (10+210), 7.72 (10+230 etc.) and 8.38 (3 zones), and Bellegarde is ~10.5. The Swiss products on the Coppet and Nyon screens have their own ratios (for example 1278 / 142 = 9.0).
- **The shop's "pre-selected" offer is not always the cheapest.** At Valleiry and Bellegarde the TER point-to-point route section is cheaper. At Coppet and Nyon the Swiss point-to-point is cheaper than the modular.

**Whether unireso alone is offered next to Léman Pass**
- On the French side, no. Every French-origin screen offered only Léman Pass products (plus Swiss ones on the Évian boat route, and the 1-month GA).
- On the Swiss side (Coppet, Nyon), no Léman Pass is offered. There, zone 10 appears only as a Swiss Modular Travelcard add-on ("incl. Genève"), next to a Mobilis zone ("incl. Coppet" 22, "incl. Nyon" 20). No standalone unireso or Mobilis pass appeared.

**Annemasse → Nyon** is sold only as Swiss products: Modular incl. Nyon 2454.00 / 274.00 and Point-to-point 2322.00 / 258.00. There is no Léman Pass, and no split into a Léman Pass part plus a Swiss part is shown.

**Categories**
- Child (6-15.99), youth (16-24.99) and Adult 25-25.99 all price as "Youth 6-25.99".
- Seniors get **no** reduction: the ticket line says "Adults".
- The youth reduction is not one ratio: about 0.79–0.80 on annual and 0.69 on monthly.
- 1st class is 1.70 × adult (zone 210) and about 1.64 × (zone 230).

**What the shop could not price**
Every one of the 24 trips got at least one offer, so none is unpriceable. The gaps are:
- No Multizones product for Valleiry (zone 240 by the brief's list), so it is route-priced only.
- No zone 200 / 210 / 230 / 240 / 250 pass without zone 10.
- No standalone unireso price on the French side.
- Discount cards are only in the "Add passenger" dialog, which asks for personal data, so they were not checked.

**Data quirks**
- Bellegarde 914.90 annual vs 87.50 monthly is a 10.5 ratio. It is recorded as shown.
- The Évian alternatives include a Swiss boat route via Lausanne.
- Page loads sometimes needed 7–15 s before the offers appeared. One monthly load (trip 2) showed no 1-month GA panel when others did.
