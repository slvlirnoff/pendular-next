## What stood out

- **1 zone and 2 zones cost the same.** Onde Verte and Libero have no 1-zone pass. The smallest pass is "1-2 Zones" and lists two zones (Onde Verte 10, 11; Libero 100, 101), even where the trip stays in one zone (Auvernier, Bern Wankdorf). ZVV has the same minimum: "1-2 Zones"/"2 Zones" for zone 110 alone.
- **ZVV counts zone 110 (Zürich city) as 2 zones.** Uster lists 4 zone numbers but is a "5 Zones" pass, and Dietikon lists 2 zone numbers but is a "3 Zones" pass.
- **Libero 2027 changed only at the top end.** 1–7 zones are identical to your 2026 figures (738 … 2,592). 8 zones went 2,700 → 2,772, 9 zones 2,790 → 2,862, and all zones 2,835 → 2,925.
- **Onde Verte went up at every level:** 684 → 711, 990 → 1,026, 1,278 → 1,314, 1,548 → 1,620. Couvet confirms the 4-zone price seen from Fleurier (1,314 / 146).
- **Monthly × 9 = annual** for every Onde Verte and Libero price, in all categories and classes. **ZVV does not follow this:** 840 vs 91 × 9 = 819, 1,985 vs 1,935, 2,363 vs 2,304, 1,349 vs 1,305.
- **Zone counts that differ from "expect":**
  - Aarberg's cheapest pass is 5 zones ("via Bus" or "via Lyss"), not 6.
  - Kerzers is 6 zones, not 7.
  - Solothurn is 8 zones direct (2,772), not all zones.
- **Variant labels and zone lists do not always match.** "6 Zones via Lyss o Wohlen o Seedorf" lists 7 zone numbers, and "7 Zones via Kerzers o Lyss" lists 9. The shop's label seems to count zones per route alternative, not the union.
- **St-Imier** is all zones at the same price as La Chaux-de-Fonds (1,620 / 180). The shop shows no zone list or count for all-zones passes.
- **Reduced categories:** youth, senior and child pay the same price, about 74.6 % of adult in Onde Verte and about 75.2 % in Libero. Seniors get no reduced 1-month GA (465, same as adults); youth and child do (310). 1st class is 1.70× (Onde Verte) and 1.65× (Libero) the 2nd-class adult price.
- **Odd detour products:** for Boudry and St-Imier the shop also offers Modular/Point-to-point cards "via Yverdon" and "via Biel", far above the network pass.
- **Shop behaviour:**
  - The class is kept in the URL (`qualityOfService=FIRST|SECOND`); the age group is not. After changing only the age group, the offers did not refresh until a class or duration toggle was clicked. Each part C price was read only after the "1x …" line showed the right category.
  - The shop accepts only these start dates: 11.11.2026 – 11.01.2027.
