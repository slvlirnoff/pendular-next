## What stood out

- **Onde Verte prices are higher than ours (trips 5 and 6).** The shop's 2027 prices are 1,620 / 180 for all zones and 1,314 / 146 for 4 zones. Ours are 1,548 / 172 and 1,278 / 142. The zones match. Our Onde Verte figures may be the 2026 prices. I did not check the shop at a 2026 date for Onde Verte.
- **ZVV (trip 9) has no zone-count product.** For Zürich HB → Winterthur the shop sells only all-zones products: the ZVV 9 o'clock Pass (proposed first, 1,349 / 145) and the ZVV NetworkPass (2,363 / 256). It shows no zone list, so the 6 zones could not be checked.
- **Libero (trip 10)** keeps 8 zones (100, 101, 115, 126, 626, 700, 701, 710), and the 2027 price is 2,772 / 308.
- **Mobilis Yverdon → Lausanne (trip 7) is 10 zones**, CHF 2,475. The 9-zone / 2,376 table is out of date.
- **Lucens → Moudon (trip 8)**: Mobilis only, no Frimobil. The shop also offers a 4-zone "via Bussy-bus-Forel" variant.
- **Lovatens → Fribourg (trip 15)**: although Lovatens is in canton Vaud, the shop proposes **Frimobil 6 zones** routed "via Prévonloup, poste – Romont FR" (zones 10, 11, 35, 36, 44, 45).
- **Lausanne → Bern (trip 13)**: the route pass costs **3,537**, not 3,465. The shop shows no tariff km. It proposes a Modular Travelcard "incl. Lausanne" (zones 11, 12) for 3,532, which is 5 CHF cheaper than the route pass.
- **Trips that leave a network (11–15)**: the shop always proposes a Modular Travelcard first (or Frimobil for trip 15), but on trips 11, 12, 14 and 15 the Point-to-point Travelcard is cheaper. On trip 13 the Modular card is 5 CHF cheaper. Each Modular variant names the one network area it adds ("incl. Lausanne", "incl. Bern", "incl. Bulle") with its zone numbers.
- **No price breakdown.** The offer screen shows only one total per variant. The "More information" popovers describe the product, not how the price is built. Any breakdown would come after "Continue", which needs a SwissPass sign-in and identity check, so I stopped there.
- **Every trip could be priced.** There were no errors, captchas or blocks.
- The 1-month GA Travelcard is CHF 465 from 04.01.2027 (CHF 440 for today's date). Trip 1 at today's date (11.11.2026) showed monthly 195 / all zones 270, against 201 / 279 from 04.01.2027.
- Product names the shop uses: "Frimobil / unireso / Onde Verte / Mobilis / Libero Annual (Monthly) Season Ticket", "Modular Travelcard", "Point-to-point Travelcard", "<network> FlexiAbo 100 Days", "ZVV 9 o'clock Pass", "ZVV NetworkPass", "1-month GA Travelcard".
